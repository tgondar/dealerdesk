DEALERDESK 2.4 - WHAT IS NEW FOR THE SHOWROOMS
==============================================

This is the text for the email to the showrooms.

1. FLEET CUSTOMERS

   A customer can now be a fleet customer. Fleet customers get 4% off the
   list price automatically. Delivery and registration are not discounted.
   The quote and the invoice preview show it on its own line:

       Fleet discount                -439.80

   You no longer need the salesman discount for this. It stays free for
   negotiating, with the same limit as before.

   To make an existing customer a fleet customer (Greenway Taxis, for
   example):
       Customers > 4. Fleet customer on / off > customer ID > Y

   When adding a new customer, the question is now
       Trade customer (Y/N, or F for fleet)?
   Y and N do what they always did. Type F for a fleet customer.

   A customer is retail, trade or fleet. A trade customer cannot also be a
   fleet customer.

2. CUSTOMERS AND VEHICLES SAVED WITH AN EMPTY BOX

   Until now, a customer saved without a phone number or an email, or a
   vehicle saved without a make or a model, came back wrong the next time
   the program was started: a trade customer showed as Retail and lost the
   trade discount, and a vehicle showed a price of 0.00.

   This is fixed. Some of these customers and vehicles will show their
   correct details again after this update. Others were saved again while
   they were wrong and cannot be recognised by the program.

   PLEASE CHECK after updating:
   - Customers > 1. List customers: any trade customer shown as Retail,
     or an email shown in the PHONE column
   - Stock > 2. List all vehicles: any vehicle with a price of 0.00

3. NOTHING ELSE CHANGES

   Stock, quotes, sales and invoices for retail and trade customers work
   exactly as in 2.3, to the penny. Your data files are used as they are.

If something is wrong after the update, tell the person who installed it.
The previous version can be put back and your data stays valid.
